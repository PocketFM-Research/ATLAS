Work in ./new_synthetic_results and do this for each of the 21 stories.

Write and run a Python script that strips all non-narrative
content. Do NOT rewrite or regenerate any text yourself. The
script must only delete regions and leave remaining prose
byte-identical.

REMOVE
1. Everything before the line matching:
   *** START OF THE PROJECT GUTENBERG EBOOK ... ***
   and everything after:
   *** END OF THE PROJECT GUTENBERG EBOOK ... ***
   Handle variants: "THIS PROJECT GUTENBERG EBOOK", differing
   asterisk counts, and older files where the marker is
   "*END*THE SMALL PRINT!" or similar.

2. Front matter INSIDE those markers:
   - title page / author / publisher / edition lines
   - table of contents (a run of short lines, often with page
     numbers or chapter names, no sentence punctuation)
   - preface, foreword, introduction, translator's note,
     dedication, epigraph attributed to a real person
   - list of illustrations, list of characters
   Narrative beginning at "CHAPTER I" or equivalent is the start
   of the story. Keep everything from there.

3. Back matter: transcriber's notes, footnote sections,
   appendices, advertisements, "Other books by this author".

4. Inline artifacts:
   - illustration captions, e.g. [Illustration: ...]
   - standalone page-number lines
   - footnote markers and their bracketed text
   - running headers repeated on every page

DO NOT REMOVE
- chapter headings (splitting depends on them)
- epigraphs that are part of the fiction
- any narrative prose, even if oddly formatted

ALSO
- normalize encoding to UTF-8, fix mojibake
- collapse 3+ blank lines to 2, strip trailing spaces, keep
  paragraph breaks
- pick one line-break policy and apply it to every story

OUTPUT per story folder:
   cleaned.txt
   clean_report.json:
     {"story_id": "...", "raw_word_count": int,
      "cleaned_word_count": int, "pct_removed": float,
      "start_marker_found": bool, "end_marker_found": bool,
      "front_matter_removed": ["table of contents", "preface"],
      "first_200_chars": "...", "last_200_chars": "..."}

Print a summary table and FLAG for manual review any story where:
- pct_removed > 25% or < 2%
- a marker was not found
- first_200_chars does not look like narrative or a chapter heading
