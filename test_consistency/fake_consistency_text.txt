Scene 1: The Market

Marcus was alive and walking through the market early in the morning.
He stopped at the bread stall and bought a small loaf.
Elena was alone in the house on the far side of town.
She sat at the kitchen table and read a letter that had arrived overnight.

Scene 2: The Alley

Marcus was dead in the alley behind the bakery.
A constable found him just after noon.
No one in the market had heard anything.

Scene 3: The Empty House

The house where Elena lived stood quiet all afternoon.
Nobody came to the door.
The letter was still open on the kitchen table.

Scene 4: The Station

Marcus arrived at the station and greeted Elena.
Marcus was alive and carrying a small traveling case.
Elena was together with Marcus on the platform.
Marcus and Elena stood side by side while the train was announced.

Scene 5: The Journey

Elena left the platform with her husband.
She was together with him in the carriage.
The train pulled away toward the coast.
Marcus was alive and looking out the window.

Scene 6: The Empty Market

The market stalls were closed by evening.
The bread stall had no customers left.
A light rain began to fall across the empty square.
