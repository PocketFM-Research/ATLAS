Scene 1: The Blue Notebook

Alex walked into the laboratory early in the morning.
He opened the drawer beneath his desk and placed the blue notebook inside.
"No one will find it here," he said quietly.
The overhead lights flickered once.
He locked the drawer, pocketed the key, and left.

Scene 2: The River Conversation

Alex stood beside the river and called his sister.
"I need to apologize," he said. "I should have told you sooner."
His sister was silent for a long moment.
"It's too late for that," she finally said.
Alex stared at the water moving past him and said nothing more.
He hung up and walked back toward the city alone.

Scene 3: The Blue Notebook Again

Alex entered the laboratory in the early morning hours.
He slid open the drawer under his desk and tucked the blue notebook inside.
"Nobody will find it there," he said, almost to himself.
The lights overhead flickered.
He locked the drawer, put the key in his pocket, and walked out.

Scene 4: The Confrontation

Sarah stopped Alex in the hallway outside the lab.
"I know what you did," she said.
Alex did not look at her. "You don't know anything."
"The notebook. I saw you put it in the drawer."
Alex kept walking. "Stay out of this, Sarah."
She watched him go, then turned and picked up her phone.

Scene 5: By the River Again

Alex was standing by the river when he called his sister.
"I owe you an apology," he said. "I kept things from you that I shouldn't have."
There was a long silence before she responded.
"You're too late," she told him.
He looked at the river running past and had nothing left to say.
He ended the call and made his way back to the city on his own.

Scene 6: The Last Conversation

"I love you," Alex said at dinner.
"I know," she replied, not looking up from her plate.
Outside the window the city hummed quietly.
He reached across the table.
She did not take his hand.
"Good night, Alex," she said, and stood up.
